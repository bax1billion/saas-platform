# Writing Your First Posts

A placeholder guide from the white-label foundation — replace it alongside
`hello-world.mdx`.

## Suggested launch content

1. **Problem posts** — articulate the pain your product removes, in the words
   your buyers use when they search.
2. **How-to posts** — practical guidance your audience wants even without
   your product; these earn trust and rankings.
3. **Comparison posts** — your product versus the status quo (usually
   spreadsheets and email).

## Mechanics

Each `.mdx` file in `content/blog/` becomes a route at `/blog/<filename>`.
Frontmatter drives the listing card, metadata, and JSON-LD. The author
default for new products lives in `config/site.ts` (`siteConfig.blog.authorName`) —
keep frontmatter `author` in sync or extend `lib/blog.ts` to fall back to it.
